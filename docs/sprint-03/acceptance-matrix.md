## Final Product Owner Decision

Sprint 03 is **ACCEPTED WITH RECORDED LIMITATIONS** under [SAR-003 v1.2](<../07-release/SAR-003 v1.2 Sprint 03 Product Acceptance.md>). The genuine Delivery DNA collection, automatic analysis, Snapshot, restart action and premium Snapshot experience gates are closed. PDR-003-004 v1.2 and PDR-003-004A v1.0.1 retain the Saved Snapshot and paid Overview boundary while fixing the launch checkout at a £295 subtotal, £0 VAT and £295 customer total.

Live payment activation remains conditional on configured Stripe credentials, verified webhook, the approved £295 provider price reference and one authorised purchase-to-result smoke. Until then the Saved Snapshot and offer remain safe and the purchase action is unavailable. Delivery DNA Action remains deferred; its earlier pre-first-entitlement condition is preserved but is not part of this Overview release. The inherited repository-wide lint baseline remains recorded debt and PDR-003-002 must not be weakened.
