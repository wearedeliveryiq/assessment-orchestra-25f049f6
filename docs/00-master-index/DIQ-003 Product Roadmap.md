| Delivery DNA Perspectives | Price and rules to be approved | Multi-stakeholder aggregation, alignment and divergence       |
| Delivery DNA Action       | Later commercial definition    | Complete action and reassessment experience                   |
| Enterprise                | Bespoke, later                 | Multi-team synthesis and advisory support                     |

The five-domain, fifteen-capability and 45-question Delivery DNA 2.0 model is locked under DIQ-100/A/B v2.0 and is implementation authority. The live thirteen-capability/39-question model remains the production authority only until Engineering completes the controlled 2.0 cutover and release evidence.

## Roadmap rules

- Delivery DNA is assembled from the platform, not rebuilt separately.
- DIQ-202 is a release gate for material changes.
- Detailed delivery activity belongs in the controlled sprint playbooks.
- Release evidence must show alignment with DIQ-002.
- Immediate work prioritises making the complete Delivery DNA 2.0 Snapshot, Saved Snapshot and Overview proposition world-class before customer acquisition or further expansion.
- Existing £295 commerce foundations remain protected; live activation resumes against the approved 2.0 journey rather than driving the product definition.
- Prices and offers are configuration-driven and historical purchases retain their pinned offer version.
- Customer-facing industry context is not benchmarking and never changes the customer's score or recommendation ranking.
