## Vision

Organisations consistently make better, faster and more confident decisions about how they deliver change.

## Mission

DeliveryIQ helps organisations build, improve and automate project delivery by turning structured evidence into explainable intelligence, prioritised action, deeper diagnosis and sustained execution support.

## Brand promise

**DeliveryIQ — Smarter project delivery.**

## Intended outcomes

- Understand delivery capability and confidence.
- Identify strengths, weaknesses and systemic patterns.
- Decide what to improve next and why.
- Investigate priority areas through specialist Knowledge Packs.
- Implement and sustain improvements with TeamMates.
- Track progress through evidence and measurable outcomes.

## Strategic boundaries

DeliveryIQ enables better delivery decisions rather than selling questionnaires. Intelligence must be evidence-based, repeatable and explainable. One shared platform powers Delivery DNA, DeliveryIQ Workspace and TeamMates.
