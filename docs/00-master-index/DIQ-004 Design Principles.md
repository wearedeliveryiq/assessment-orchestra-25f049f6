## Principles

1. Start with the user decision or delivery outcome.
2. Reuse one shared platform across every experience.
3. Make material intelligence explainable and traceable.
4. Ask only for evidence needed to produce a defensible outcome.
5. Separate evidence interpretation, action, deeper diagnosis and execution support.
6. Expose capabilities progressively according to product mode and permission.
7. Show confidence and evidence quality rather than false certainty.
8. Keep humans accountable for consequential decisions and actions.
9. Extend through governed contracts rather than parallel logic.
10. Define and measure intended outcomes.

## Decision test

A design must identify its user and decision, required evidence, shared capability, explanation path, permissions, oversight and measurable result.
