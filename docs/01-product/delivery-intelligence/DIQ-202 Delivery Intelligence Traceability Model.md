## Purpose

This is the architectural contract tracing customer evidence through capability, score, intelligence, confidence, recommendation, Knowledge Pack, TeamMate and measured outcome.

## Minimum traceability record

Evidence ID and version; capability domain; scoring rule; intelligence output; confidence rule; recommendation ID; Knowledge Pack mapping; TeamMate mapping; outcome measure.

## Specification outline

1. Identifiers and versioning.
2. Evidence-to-capability mappings.
3. Score-to-intelligence rules.
4. Intelligence-to-recommendation triggers.
5. Knowledge Pack entry criteria.
6. TeamMate eligibility criteria.
7. Narrative lineage and confidence.
8. Coverage and broken-link reporting.
9. Change-impact analysis.
10. Release evidence and sign-off.

## Release gate

Relevant changes require demonstrated coverage, traceability, actionability, consistency and explainability. Orphaned evidence, conclusions, recommendations or mappings block release.
