## Purpose

The engine transforms assessment evidence into explainable delivery intelligence and is DeliveryIQ's central intellectual property.

## Responsibilities

Capability analysis, maturity and confidence scoring, pattern recognition, executive insight, strengths, weaknesses and downstream recommendation triggers.

## Specification outline

1. Canonical inputs and evidence schema.
2. Capability and maturity models.
3. Scoring, weighting and aggregation.
4. Confidence, coverage and evidence quality.
5. Pattern, strength, weakness and root-cause logic.
6. Narrative and executive insight.
7. Downstream output contracts.
8. Determinism, explainability, versioning and audit.
9. Benchmarking and historical comparison.
10. Validation and acceptance thresholds.

## Release gates

Controlled identical inputs produce repeatable outputs; conclusions trace through DIQ-202; weak evidence lowers confidence; model changes are versioned and impact-assessed.
