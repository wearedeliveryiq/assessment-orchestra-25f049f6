## Purpose

The framework converts Delivery Intelligence into prioritised action.

## Responsibilities

Improvements, impact and effort, quick wins, strategic initiatives, implementation guidance, sequencing, 30/60/90-day roadmaps and success measures.

## Specification outline

1. Recommendation object and taxonomy.
2. Eligibility, suppression and conflict rules.
3. Impact, effort, urgency, confidence and priority.
4. Quick-win and strategic classifications.
5. Sequencing, dependencies and roadmap construction.
6. Guidance and accountable roles.
7. Success measures and outcome tracking.
8. Knowledge Pack and TeamMate mappings.
9. Human override and audit.
10. Acceptance criteria.

Every recommendation must state its evidence, trigger, rationale, confidence, impact, effort, sequence, success measure and related Pack or TeamMate.
