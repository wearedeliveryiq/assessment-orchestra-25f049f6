## Purpose

The framework supplies specialist diagnostic content running through the shared platform.

Each pack contains questions, evidence rules, scoring, confidence, patterns, recommendations, narrative guidance, entry criteria and completion criteria.

## Specification outline

1. Pack manifest, ownership, version and lifecycle.
2. Recommendation and entry criteria.
3. Evidence, scoring and confidence contracts.
4. Patterns, root causes and narratives.
5. Recommendation and TeamMate mappings.
6. Authoring, review, testing and approval.
7. Content, accessibility and localisation standards.
8. Product-mode execution rules.
9. Effectiveness and retirement rules.
10. Acceptance tests and release gates.

Initial subjects include Executive Sponsorship, Governance, PMO Effectiveness, Benefits, Portfolio, Planning and Controls, Risk, P3M3, Agile Delivery and Change Management.
