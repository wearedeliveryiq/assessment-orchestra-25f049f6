## 1. Purpose and unchanged product model

Delivery DNA remains a scale-neutral, method-neutral assessment of how an organisation—or a clearly defined part of it—directs, governs, delivers, enables and improves project-based change.

Unless amended below, the following locked v2.0 product rules remain unchanged and are incorporated into v2.1:

- supported organisational scopes and the single lead-perspective disclosure;
- five domains and fifteen capabilities;
- Emerging, Developing, Established and Leading response levels;
- reason-required Not applicable and non-contributing missing/excluded evidence;
- Snapshot result boundary, five-domain spider chart and Saved Snapshot value exchange;
- Overview scoring transformation, bands, availability, confidence, findings, patterns, recommendations and bounded roadmap;
- customer-safe explainability, contextual-evidence isolation and report boundary;
- £295 one-off Overview position, no-VAT treatment and payment integrity;
- privacy, tenant isolation, traceability, immutable history and fail-closed cutover; and
- Perspectives, Action and Enterprise as later products.

DIQ-100A v2.1.0 is complete machine authority where this document summarises a rule.

## 4. Response and scoring rules

Customers choose one behavioural statement, not a number:

1. Emerging;
2. Developing;
3. Established;
4. Leading; or
5. Not applicable, with a mandatory reason.

Question values transform to Overview scores using `(answer - 1) / 3 * 100`. Capability scores use the renormalised weighted mean of eligible answers. A capability requires at least two eligible answers and at least 60% eligible weight. Missing, Not applicable and excluded responses never become zero.

Domain, overall, threshold, precision, rounding, confidence, finding, pattern, recommendation and roadmap rules remain exactly as defined in DIQ-100A v2.1.0 and its golden fixtures.

## 5. Snapshot

The Snapshot uses exactly the fifteen questions whose role is `snapshot`. It remains anonymous, no-PII and normally six to eight minutes.

The premium interaction and presentation principles first approved in PDR-003-005/A v1.1 are incorporated here, but their thirteen-question Delivery DNA 1.0 content and machine configuration are superseded for new journeys.

It provides:

- one non-numeric overall indicative level;
- five non-numeric indicative domain levels;
- an accessible five-axis spider chart;
- up to two positive signals and two areas to explore;
- no more than one approved contextual-evidence item;
- a truthful preparation transition; and
- **Save my Snapshot**, supported by **Download your results by saving your Snapshot**.

The mandatory caveat is:

> This is an indicative view based on one Snapshot signal from each Delivery DNA capability. It is not your complete Delivery DNA result and may change when the supporting questions are assessed.

Saved Snapshot adds only download of the same result, private return access, retention of the fifteen exact answers and continuation without repetition. It adds no diagnosis and creates no analysis run.

## 6. Overview

Following verified £295 access, the customer completes the thirty supporting questions. The Overview provides the complete authorised five-domain and fifteen-capability result, confidence and limitations, findings, eligible patterns, top three recommendations, bounded 30/60/90 direction, source-safe industry context, explainability and seven-section report.

Snapshot answers carry forward exactly under the same v2.1 question IDs and remain editable before completion. Registration, a redirect or a client-supplied payment value never grants Overview access or starts analysis.
