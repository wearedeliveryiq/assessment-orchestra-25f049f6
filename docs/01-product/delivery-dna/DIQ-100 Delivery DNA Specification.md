## Purpose

Delivery DNA is the flagship public entry product and a controlled public slice of DeliveryIQ.

## Scope

It gathers concise evidence, provides a high-level capability view, identifies strengths and priorities, recommends Knowledge Packs, previews TeamMates and creates a route into the Workspace.

It uses the shared assessment runtime, evidence model, Intelligence Engine, Recommendation Framework, Knowledge Pack logic and TeamMate mappings. It excludes full organisational, historical, portfolio, audit and active TeamMate features.

## Specification outline

1. Personas, entry routes and conversion outcomes.
2. Capability domains and assessment scope.
3. Question-selection and evidence rules.
4. Scoring, confidence and evidence thresholds.
5. Results, narrative and presentation.
6. Knowledge Pack and TeamMate previews.
7. Identity, consent, privacy and conversion rules.
8. Accessibility, performance and security.
9. Analytics and success measures.
10. Acceptance tests and release gates.

## Architectural acceptance criteria

No duplicate core logic; every conclusion is explainable; every recommendation is traceable through DIQ-202; public access exposes only authorised capabilities and data.
