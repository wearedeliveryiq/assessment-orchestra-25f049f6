## Decision

The approved £295 one-off, no-VAT Delivery DNA Overview offer applies to Delivery DNA 2.1 rather than v2.0. All payment-integrity, tenant, privacy, idempotency, immutable-history, customer-copy and projection boundaries in PDR-003-004 v2.0 remain unchanged except for the following versioned scope:

- question-set and assessment version `2.1.0`;
- configuration ID `delivery-dna-product-config-2.1.0`;
- fifteen v2.1 Snapshot questions and thirty v2.1 supporting questions;
- no translation or access carry-over from a v2.0 Snapshot; and
- new v2.0 checkout and collection disabled at v2.1 cutover.

The production preflight found zero checkouts, payment events, access grants or v2.0 analysis runs. No commercial migration, refund or fulfilment remediation is required. Existing v2.0 test/internal records remain immutable and cannot qualify for v2.1 fulfilment.

Checkout stays fail-closed until provider credentials, the approved £295 price reference and an authorised purchase-to-result smoke are available. Customer promotion resumes only after Product Owner visual acceptance of the exact v2.1 production wording and Snapshot result.

## Acceptance

Engineering must retain all PDR-003-004A v2.1 fixtures and prove that a v2.0 Snapshot, checkout reference, redirect or payment event cannot grant v2.1 access. No other commercial behaviour changes.
