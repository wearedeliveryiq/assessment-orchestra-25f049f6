## 4. Recovery Acceptance Decision

### 4.1 Former blocker disposition

The former recovery blocker is closed by an approved change of requirement, not by technical proof:

- the Tier 1 RPO <=15 minutes requirement is removed for the current stage;
- the Tier 1 RTO <=4 hours requirement is removed for the current stage;
- arbitrary point-in-time and isolated-restore requirements are removed for the current stage;
- PDR-004-002's Route A/Route B remediation decision is superseded; and
- no recovery pass is inferred from automated tests, daily backups, exports or documentation.

### 4.2 Accepted current recovery position

The accepted position is:

- Lovable documents daily database backups and roughly 14-day retention;
- restore is in place to an available daily snapshot;
- changes after the chosen snapshot may be lost;
- the database is unavailable while restore runs;
- an older restored schema may require application reconciliation;
- arbitrary point-in-time and isolated self-service restore are unavailable in the documented control;
- database backup does not cover storage-bucket files;
- logical export is a limited portability control, not a managed restore; and
- no fixed recovery point or recovery time is promised.

### 4.3 Minimum operational evidence

The current Sprint evidence satisfies the amended documentary acceptance gate through the authoritative capability assessment, project read-only assessment, controlled policy, release plan, named Head of Software ownership and explicit Matt Prust risk acceptance.

Before customer enablement under the accepted Sprint 04 release, the Head of Software must confirm and record that a recent project backup is visible and the recovery runbook is operationally accessible. This is a pre-enablement operational gate, not evidence that a restore occurred. Monthly checks apply while customer data is present, as defined by PDR-004-001 v1.1.

## 5. Recorded Limitations and Residual Risks

### L-004-01 — Genuine eligible Delivery DNA journey unavailable

**Accepted Sprint limitation; complete-journey general-availability blocker.** A genuine eligible Delivery DNA portfolio is unavailable, so the complete production customer journey has not run. No synthetic customer evidence is authorised to bypass this gate.

### L-004-02 — Inherited repository-wide lint debt

**Accepted inherited debt; not a Sprint blocker.** The recorded 613 errors and 15 warnings remain outside changed scope. Changed files, type checking and production build pass. The baseline must not increase and must not be represented as clean.

### L-004-03 — Audit export not exercised on genuine evidence

**Accepted contained limitation; audit-export enablement blocker.** Export implementation and automated controls pass, but production has no genuine portfolio. `audit_exports` must remain disabled until an authorised genuine export verifies projection, redaction, expiry and access logging.

### L-004-04 — Proportionate recovery residual risk

**Explicitly accepted by Matt Prust for the current product stage.** DeliveryIQ may lose changes since the selected available daily snapshot; restore is in place; downtime and recovery duration are unmeasured; schema reconciliation may be required; storage and other excluded components need separate treatment; and no fixed RPO/RTO, point-in-time or isolated-restore promise is made.

This risk acceptance must be reviewed when customer commitments, regulation, data value, product maturity, platform capability or the cost/risk balance changes materially.
