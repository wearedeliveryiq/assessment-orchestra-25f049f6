## 1. Product outcome

The Delivery DNA Snapshot must create a premium, credible first interaction with DeliveryIQ. It must give the customer a clear result moment and an obvious next step without presenting the thirteen practice responses as the complete Delivery DNA diagnosis.

The governing proposition remains:

> 13 questions to engage. 39 questions to diagnose.

The Snapshot output is expanded from two directional card lists to:

1. an **indicative delivery maturity** level;
2. an indicative thirteen-capability practice-signal profile;
3. a concise interpretation;
4. up to two positive signals and two areas to explore; and
5. a prominent continuation into the complete Delivery DNA Assessment.

## 3. Indicative delivery maturity

### 3.1 Customer presentation

The exact label is **Your indicative delivery maturity**. Display one of:

| ID            | Customer label | Interpretation                                                                               |
| ------------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `emerging`    | Emerging       | Delivery practices appear to be at an early or inconsistent stage across the areas assessed. |
| `developing`  | Developing     | Some delivery practices are taking shape, but consistency varies across the areas assessed.  |
| `established` | Established    | Delivery practices appear consistently established across many of the areas assessed.        |
| `leading`     | Leading        | The responses indicate strong consistency across the delivery practices assessed.            |

Do not display the underlying mean, a numeric score, percentage, percentile or the authoritative DIQ-203 band.

### 3.2 Deterministic calculation

- Use only answered 1–5 responses.
- Exclude `not_applicable`; never treat it as zero, neutral or missing.
- Give each answered Snapshot question equal weight.
- Calculate the unrounded arithmetic mean to at least six decimal places.
- Apply lower-inclusive boundaries to the unrounded mean:

| Answer mean       | Indicative level |
| ----------------- | ---------------- |
| `1.00 ≤ x < 2.50` | `emerging`       |
| `2.50 ≤ x < 3.50` | `developing`     |
| `3.50 ≤ x < 4.25` | `established`    |
| `4.25 ≤ x ≤ 5.00` | `leading`        |

The level is unavailable unless the existing result-admissibility rules pass. It is a separate Snapshot presentation rule and must not invoke or reuse the DIQ-203 overall scorer.

### 3.3 Mandatory caveat

> This is an indicative view based on the 13 practices covered by your Snapshot. It is not your complete Delivery DNA result and may change when foundations and supporting evidence are assessed.

Do not use **Delivery DNA maturity score**, **authoritative maturity**, **benchmark**, **prediction** or claims of comparative performance.

## 4. Indicative delivery-profile chart

The result includes a premium spider/radar visual titled **Your indicative delivery profile** with supporting text **Based on one practice signal from each Delivery DNA capability.**

Rules:

1. Use exactly thirteen axes in DIQ-203A capability order.
2. Each answered axis plots its raw 1–5 response linearly; do not apply weights or aggregate it with another question.
3. `not_applicable` is a `null` axis, rendered as a visible gap and marked **N/A**. It is never plotted as zero or neutral.
4. Do not show numeric capability scores, percentages, benchmarks, confidence or comparison data.
5. The chart is labelled as an indicative **practice-signal profile**, not a capability score profile.
6. Provide full labels on interaction and an equivalent accessible list containing capability label and selected response label or N/A.
7. At narrow widths, use numbered axes with a corresponding ordered legend or an equivalent readable profile visual using the same values.
8. The chart must remain understandable without colour.

## 5. Question interaction

Normal answered responses auto-advance to reduce friction:

1. Selecting a 1–5 response shows a clear selected state for approximately 350 milliseconds.
2. Persist the answer successfully before changing question.
3. Advance automatically only after persistence succeeds.
4. Keep **Back** visible and allow any previous response to be changed.
5. If persistence fails, remain on the current question, retain the selection locally and show a safe retry state.
6. Selecting **Not applicable** opens the mandatory reason input and advances only after the valid reason is explicitly continued and saved.
7. Pointer/touch selection and explicit keyboard activation with Enter, Space or approved number shortcut may auto-advance. Arrow-key radio traversal must not commit or advance until explicit activation.
8. After advance, move focus to the next question heading and announce the new question number accessibly.
9. A successfully saved thirteenth response enters the preparation transition automatically; no redundant Next button is required.

Interrupted-session resume, idempotency and safe replay remain unchanged.

## 6. Branded preparation transition

After the thirteenth response and before the result, show a purposeful preparation state for a target of four to six seconds.

**Heading:** Building your Delivery DNA Snapshot

**Supporting text:** We’re reviewing the patterns across your 13 responses.

Progressively present these truthful steps:

1. Reviewing your delivery-practice responses
2. Identifying positive signals
3. Highlighting areas to explore
4. Determining your indicative maturity
5. Preparing your personalised Snapshot

Then reveal **Your Snapshot is ready** and transition to the result.

The transition may use the approved DeliveryIQ ribbon mark with restrained movement and glow. It must not claim AI analysis, benchmarking, external-data comparison, evidence validation or use of the full Delivery Intelligence Engine. If real processing exceeds six seconds, show **Still preparing your Snapshot** rather than freezing or falsely reporting completion. Reduced-motion settings must replace movement with static status changes.

## 7. Results hierarchy and conversion

Render results in this order:

1. branded completion hero;
2. **Your indicative delivery maturity** and level;
3. mandatory caveat;
4. **Your indicative delivery profile** chart;
5. level-specific interpretation;
6. up to two positive signals;
7. up to two areas to explore;
8. continuation panel; and
9. quiet tertiary **Start a new Snapshot** action.

The continuation panel uses:

**Heading:** Turn your Snapshot into your complete Delivery DNA

**Body:** You’ve already completed 13 of 39 questions. Create your free account to carry these answers forward and discover your complete capability profile, priority recommendations and personalised roadmap preview.

**Primary action:** Complete my Delivery DNA

The primary action uses the existing verified registration and explicit-consent continuation. After linking, the authenticated equivalent is **Continue my Delivery DNA**. The restart action must not compete visually with the primary continuation.
