## 1. Product and commercial decision

DeliveryIQ will optimise the complete acquisition and first-purchase journey before expanding improvement tracking or enterprise workflows.

The approved customer ladder is:

| Stage                     |  Current price | Product outcome                                         | Status                           |
| ------------------------- | -------------: | ------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Delivery DNA Snapshot     |             £0 | Anonymous indicative Snapshot                           | Live and immediate focus         |
| Saved Snapshot            |             £0 | Securely retained Snapshot and purchase path            | Immediate focus                  |
| Delivery DNA Overview     |   £295 one-off | Complete evidence-linked diagnosis and executive result | Immediate focus                  |
| Delivery DNA Perspectives | To be approved | Multi-stakeholder alignment and divergence              | Next product-definition priority |
| Delivery DNA Action       | To be approved | Ongoing implementation, tracking and reassessment       | Later roadmap                    |
| Enterprise                |        Bespoke | Multi-team synthesis and advisory support               | Later roadmap                    |

The governing value boundaries are:

> Snapshot identifies where a delivery capability issue may exist. Overview explains the complete position, confidence and priorities. Later products help multiple stakeholders align and turn priorities into sustained action.

An account is not a product tier. Customer-facing language describes the outcome **Saved Snapshot** and the action **Save my Snapshot**.

## 3. Approved customer journey

### 3.1 Delivery DNA Snapshot

The live PDR-003-005/A v1.1 experience remains free, anonymous and without PII. It provides the approved indicative maturity, indicative practice-signal profile, bounded positive signals and areas to explore.

It must not expose the complete capability scores, confidence index, engine findings, recommendations, roadmap or industry comparisons.

### 3.2 Saved Snapshot

After Snapshot completion, the primary continuation is to save the result in a secure DeliveryIQ workspace.

Registration remains free. Verified authentication and explicit consent are required before the exact Snapshot responses are linked to the current full assessment draft. The linked draft remains `in_progress`; registration does not grant Overview access, complete the assessment or request analysis.

A Saved Snapshot provides only:

- persistent access to the linked Snapshot result;
- the ability to return to the purchase journey;
- the exact current progress state; and
- clear information about the paid Overview.

It does not provide the complete assessment, complete result, capability scores, engine confidence, recommendations or roadmap.

### 3.3 Delivery DNA Overview purchase

The approved launch offer is **£295 one-off** for one Delivery DNA Overview.

One purchase is scoped to:

- one verified purchaser;
- one organisation and workspace;
- one Saved Snapshot and its linked Delivery DNA 1.0.0 assessment draft;
- completion of the remaining 26 questions;
- one immutable analysis result and its authorised Overview projections; and
- ongoing authorised read access to that purchased result under the existing retention and tenant rules.

The purchase is not a subscription, does not grant unlimited assessments, and does not grant Delivery DNA Action, Knowledge Pack or TeamMate access.

The server grants access only after a verified, idempotently processed payment-provider success event. A browser success redirect, client-supplied price, client-supplied entitlement or unverified event never grants access.

The checkout must disclose the final total and any applicable tax before the customer confirms payment. DeliveryIQ does not store card details.

### 3.4 Existing records and customers

- Existing Snapshot responses, linked drafts, completed assessments, results and analysis history remain immutable.
- A genuine customer result or full-assessment access created under PDR-003-004 v1.0 must not be removed retrospectively.
- New Saved Snapshots created after the v1.1 effective cutover require a valid Overview purchase before the remaining full-assessment journey is opened.
- Engineering must perform a production preflight and preserve any genuine existing customer access. Zero genuine affected customers requires no synthetic grant or backfill.

## 4. World-class Delivery DNA Overview

The £295 customer buys a decision-quality diagnosis, not additional questionnaire data.

### 4.1 Required web result

The Overview must present:

1. overall Delivery DNA display score and approved band;
2. complete executive summary;
3. all thirteen current capability labels, display scores/bands or unavailable state;
4. confidence index, band, evidence coverage, limitations and improvement prompts;
5. up to five strengths and five priority opportunities in the locked order;
6. the top three presented recommendations with priority, impact, effort, safe reason, expected outcome and a practical first step;
7. a high-level 30/60/90-day direction using no more than the first scheduled item in each non-empty horizon;
8. relevant approved industry context;
9. explanation of how the conclusions connect to the customer's evidence without exposing restricted trace detail; and
10. a clear distinction between immediate insight and later Action functionality.

The Overview must not expose:

- recommendations below the approved top three;
- full dependency, sequencing, override or unscheduled-item detail;
- the complete generated roadmap;
- customer decision-state controls;
- action ownership, target dates, progress or outcome tracking;
- unrestricted audit trace or respondent identity;
- separately entitled Knowledge Pack content; or
- TeamMate activation.

### 4.2 Presentation standard

The result must feel consistent with the premium Snapshot and current DeliveryIQ brand. Minimum expectations are:

- a clear executive result hierarchy with no internal administration or runtime navigation;
- strong visualisation of overall position and the capability profile;
- a readable heatmap or equivalent strength/exposure view that does not rely on colour alone;
- progressive explanation from **what we found** to **why it matters** to **what to do first**;
- excellent desktop and mobile layouts;
- WCAG 2.2 AA core journey;
- truthful low-confidence, unavailable and partial-evidence states;
- printable styling and a downloadable, board-ready executive report; and
- no display of internal rule formulas, proprietary predicates, hidden reasoning or unsupported precision.

The downloadable report must reproduce the authorised Overview projection. It must not become a second calculation path or leak fields excluded from the web projection.

### 4.3 Industry context

Industry context is governed by DIQ-204/A and may be displayed only when the evidence item is approved for customer context and materially relevant to the result.

Each item must include:

- the heading **Why this matters now** or **Industry context**;
- approved customer-safe wording;
- source publisher and evidence year;
- a link or citation to the original source where permitted;
- the applicable scope/method caveat; and
- the statement that the evidence provides context and is not a prediction of the customer's outcome.

Industry context must not:

- change scoring, confidence, findings, ranking or roadmap generation;
- claim that an industry statistic caused the customer's result;
- state that the customer is above or below similar organisations;
- imply a matched benchmark population; or
- combine unrelated studies into an invented trend.

## 5. Configurable price and offer contract

PDR-003-004A is the runtime commercial authority. Customer-facing components and APIs must resolve the active offer server-side; they must not embed `£295` as an independent business rule.

The configuration records:

- stable offer and product identifiers;
- configuration version and effective period;
- currency and minor-unit amount;
- customer display copy;
- charge type;
- tax-disclosure policy;
- purchase scope;
- entitlement/access key and version;
- included and excluded projections;
- checkout-provider price reference supplied through deployment configuration; and
- the offer version pinned to every checkout, payment and access grant.

A future price change requires a new approved offer-configuration version and operational activation. It does not require changing assessment code, modifying historical purchases or recalculating results.

## 7. Next-tier product direction

### 7.1 Delivery DNA Perspectives

Perspectives is the next product-definition priority after the Overview journey is proven in production. Its intended outcome is to show organisational agreement, divergence and evidence breadth across relevant stakeholder groups without judging named individuals.

It will require separately approved rules for participation, aggregation, weighting, minimum cohort sizes, anonymity, suppression, confidence, group comparison, pricing and report disclosure. Engineering must not infer or implement these rules from the current single-perspective model.

### 7.2 Delivery DNA 2.0 candidate

The proposed five-domain, fifteen-capability and 45-question model remains a product-design candidate. It is not approved production configuration and must not replace the locked thirteen-capability/39-question model without:

- exact taxonomy and question wording;
- Snapshot and full-assessment calculation rules;
- compatibility and migration policy;
- golden fixtures;
- customer comprehension/completion evidence; and
- Product Owner approval.

### 7.3 Delivery DNA Action and Enterprise

The existing Action foundations remain protected but receive no additional product investment until the Snapshot, Saved Snapshot and Overview purchase/result journey is operating effectively. Enterprise packaging remains founder-led and bespoke. Neither blocks the immediate work package.

## 8. Minimum acceptance evidence

Engineering must demonstrate:

1. exact replacement of customer-facing **free account** product language with the Saved Snapshot journey while retaining truthful supporting registration copy;
2. Snapshot calculation, result, privacy and all PDR-003-005A fixtures unchanged;
3. Saved Snapshot creates no complete assessment, analysis run or paid projection;
4. £295 active offer resolved from server-side configuration rather than duplicated UI constants;
5. verified payment event creates exactly one workspace/assessment-scoped Overview access grant;
6. redirect-only, failed, cancelled, duplicate, replayed, wrong-price and wrong-workspace attempts do not grant or duplicate access;
7. purchased customer can resume, complete the remaining questions and receive the same immutable engine result without commercial recalculation;
8. unpaid users cannot access the remaining assessment, Overview APIs, downloadable report or restricted fields;
9. exact Overview field allow-list and excluded Action fields at API and UI boundaries;
10. industry context uses only eligible DIQ-204A items with source/year/caveat and never changes the result;
11. web and downloadable reports reconcile to the same authorised projection;
12. responsive 390px and 1440px customer journeys, keyboard/screen-reader flow, non-colour meaning and print output;
13. existing genuine customer access preserved and zero synthetic grants when no affected customer exists;
14. tenant isolation, payment integrity, privacy, cache, entitlement revocation and audit coverage;
15. all DIQ-203B, PDR-003-005A and commercial-access regressions unchanged; and
16. type checking, changed-file lint/format, production build and hosted purchase-to-result smoke in an authorised test context.

Update the existing Sprint 03 acceptance matrix and consolidated implementation report. Add one concise commerce/operations runbook only if required to operate checkout safely. Do not create a new sprint playbook or per-component governance reports.

## 9. Implementation authority and priority

Engineering is authorised to reconcile this record and PDR-003-004A, implement the complete current-model Snapshot → Saved Snapshot → £295 Overview vertical slice, test it and deploy through the established safe path without another readiness or routine approval gate.

This work supersedes PB-005 RC1 as the immediate customer-value priority. PB-005 remains non-authoritative release-candidate material and may resume after the commercial journey is operational or when explicitly reprioritised.

Stop only for a genuine higher-authority conflict, inability to establish payment-provider access, an unresolved tax/legal checkout requirement, unexpected genuine-customer access impact, or material payment/security/tenant risk. A blocker in checkout does not prevent completion of the Saved Snapshot and world-class Overview presentation work.
